Four Pillars of Vector Calculus

From curvilinear coordinates to flux divergence and circulating vorticity, explore physical and geometric intuition through interactive 3D models.

🌐
3D CURVILINEAR

1. Coordinate Systems

Seamlessly toggle between Cartesian $(x,y,z)$, Cylindrical $(\rho,\phi,z)$, and Spherical $(r,\theta,\phi)$ spaces. Observe intersecting coordinate surfaces and understand how basis vectors change orientation in space.

  • Constant coordinate planes, cylinders, spheres, and cones
  • Dynamic local orthonormal basis vectors ($\hat{a}_i$)
  • Differential volume element ($dV = h_1 h_2 h_3 du_1 du_2 du_3$)
Launch Coordinate Systems
⛰️
SCALAR POTENTIAL

2. The Gradient ($\nabla V$)

Visualize the rate and direction of maximum increase of scalar potential hills. Watch animated particle streamlines climb paths of steepest ascent toward summits along tangent gradient vectors.

  • Single Peak Gaussian, Twin Peaks, and Volcano Crater landscapes
  • Dynamic equipotential contour rings perpendicular to gradient
  • Arbitrary scalar function evaluator $V(u_1, u_2, u_3)$ with KaTeX preview
Launch The Gradient
💧
FLUID FLUX

3. The Divergence ($\nabla \cdot \vec{A}$)

Grasp net outward flux through an intuitive fluid flow simulation. Place and configure up to 4 interactive source and sink points to observe radiant expansion, convergent drainage, or solenoidal states.

  • 4 interactive sources ($\nabla\cdot\vec{A} \gt 0$) & sinks ($\nabla\cdot\vec{A} \lt 0$)
  • Solenoidal / incompressible mode support ($\nabla\cdot\vec{A} = 0$)
  • Vector field divergence calculator across all coordinate systems
Launch The Divergence
🌀
ROTATION & SWIRL

4. The Curl ($\nabla \times \vec{A}$)

Explore rotational vorticity and paddle-wheel micro-rotation. Control 4 independent vortex centers with counter-clockwise, clockwise, or irrotational configurations complete with circulating streamlines.

  • 4 interactive vortices with CCW, CW, and zero-vorticity modes
  • KaTeX cross-product determinant HUD matrix preview
  • Conservative / irrotational state classifier and magnitude readout
Launch The Curl
