## 4. Topic Specifications & Mathematical Formulations

### 1. Coordinate Systems and Spatial Geometries (`topics/coords.js`)
- **Cartesian, Cylindrical, and Spherical**: Points defined via intersecting surfaces (planes $x, y, z$; cylinders $\rho$, half-planes $\phi$; spheres $r$, cones $\theta$).
- **Cone Geometry**: Polar angles $\theta \in (0, \pi)$ transition smoothly without singularities; flat equatorial plane at $\theta = 90^\circ$ and lower hemisphere opening for $\theta > 90^\circ$.
- **Unit Vectors $(\hat{a}_1, \hat{a}_2, \hat{a}_3)$**: Position-dependent curvilinear arrows dynamically reorienting at probe point $P$.
- **Differential Spatial Volume Element ($dv$)**: Highlighting volume scaling factors:
  - Cartesian: $dv = dx\,dy\,dz$
  - Cylindrical: $dv = \rho\,d\rho\,d\phi\,dz$
  - Spherical: $dv = r^2\sin\theta\,dr\,d\theta\,d\phi$

### 2. The Gradient ($\nabla V$) (`topics/gradient.js`)
- **Topographical Potential Hill**: 3D surface $z = V(x, y)$ in Cartesian coordinates with no probe ball.
- **Profiles**: Single Gaussian Peak ($V = A e^{-r^2/\sigma^2}$), Twin Peaks, and Volcano Crater with adjustable height and spread.
- **Surface Tangent Gradient Vectors**: 3D gradient vector arrows lie directly tangent to the surface pointing uphill, strictly perpendicular to equipotential contour rings ($V = \text{const}$).
- **Ascending Streamlines & Pulses**: Glowing animated particles flowing along the steepest ascent path towards the summit.

### 3. Divergence ($\nabla \cdot \vec{A}$) (`topics/divergence.js`)
- **Image 1 Styling**: Deep dark background, dense 2D vector field grid.
- **4 Controllable Points**: 2 Sources (q > 0) with outward radial white arrows and luminous yellow/cyan halo; 2 Sinks (q < 0) with inward radial white arrows and blue convergence.
- **Controls**: Pill selector (P1, P2, P3, P4), position sliders ($X \in [-3.0, 3.0], Y \in [-2.0, 2.0]$), Source/Sink toggle, strength slider.
- **450 Animated Streamline Particles**: Fluid particles streaming from sources and converging into sinks.

### 4. The Curl ($\nabla \times \vec{A}$) (`topics/curl.js`)
- **Image 2 Styling (3Blue1Brown Aesthetic)**: Dark background with fiery red outer region $\to$ vibrant orange $\to$ golden streamlines $\to$ cyan central streams.
- **4 Controllable Vortices**: 2 Counter-Clockwise vortices ($\text{curl } \mathbf{F} > 0$) with white loops and 3D labels; 2 Clockwise vortices ($\text{curl } \mathbf{F} < 0$) with white loops and 3D labels. Top header label `Curl (∇×F)`.
- **Controls**: Pill selector (V1, V2, V3, V4), position sliders ($X \in [-3.0, 3.0], Y \in [-2.0, 2.0]$), CCW/CW circulation toggle, strength slider.
- **480 Animated Streamline Particles**: Swirling fluid particles tracing vortex circulation lines.

## 5. Scientific Calculators Suite (`ui.js` & `math.js`)

1. **Coordinate System Converter**:
   - Converts input coordinates between Cartesian $(x,y,z)$, Cylindrical $(\rho,\phi,z)$, and Spherical $(r,\theta,\phi)$.
   - Supports both degree and radian display with full precision and KaTeX formatted equations.
2. **Unit Vector Calculator**:
   - Computes local unit vectors $(\hat{a}_1, \hat{a}_2, \hat{a}_3)$ at any user-specified point $P$.
   - Expresses curvilinear basis vectors in terms of Cartesian unit vectors $(\hat{a}_x, \hat{a}_y, \hat{a}_z)$.
3. **Magnitude Calculator**:
   - Calculates the exact 3D Euclidean magnitude $|\vec{A}| = \sqrt{A_x^2 + A_y^2 + A_z^2}$.
4. **Direction Calculator**:
   - Computes direction cosines $\cos\alpha = A_x/|\vec{A}|$, $\cos\beta = A_y/|\vec{A}|$, $\cos\gamma = A_z/|\vec{A}|$.
   - Computes coordinate angles $\alpha, \beta, \gamma \in [0^\circ, 180^\circ]$ and spherical direction angles $\theta, \phi$.
5. **Gradient Calculator**:
   - Evaluates scalar height $V(x, y)$ and gradient vector $\nabla V = \frac{\partial V}{\partial x}\hat{a}_x + \frac{\partial V}{\partial y}\hat{a}_y$ at any test point $(x, y)$.
   - Computes gradient magnitude and ascent direction angle.
6. **Divergence Calculator**:
   - Calculates total vector field $\vec{F}(x, y)$ and scalar divergence $\nabla \cdot \vec{F}$ at any point $(x, y)$ generated by the 4 plane points.
   - Classifies point as Net Source ($\nabla \cdot \vec{F} > 0$), Net Sink ($\nabla \cdot \vec{F} < 0$), or Incompressible / Solenoidal ($\nabla \cdot \vec{F} = 0$).
7. **Curl Calculator**:
   - Calculates total vector field $\vec{F}(x, y)$ and 2D curl / z-vorticity $(\nabla \times \vec{F})_z = \frac{\partial F_y}{\partial x} - \frac{\partial F_x}{\partial y}$ at any point $(x, y)$ generated by the 4 vortices.
   - Classifies local rotation as Counter-Clockwise ($\text{curl} > 0$), Clockwise ($\text{curl} < 0$), or Irrotational ($\text{curl} = 0$).
